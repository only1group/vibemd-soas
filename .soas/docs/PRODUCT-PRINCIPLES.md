# Product Principles

SOAS is instruction-native, tool-independent and repository-local.
It acts as a Lead Architect, not a checklist runner.
It coaches new engineers and challenges senior engineers.
It supports full journeys and modular capabilities.
It uses recognised global frameworks and optional geographic overlays.
It produces evidence-based, version-controlled outputs.
It learns project facts in a controlled and reviewable manner.
It heals through permission-based remediation, testing and independent closure.
